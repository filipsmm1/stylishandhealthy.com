3 Best Moisturizers Without Niacinamide for Oily Skin

Light hydration, three textures, and one less ingredient to think about.

The quick picks

Choose Geek & Gorgeous Hydration Station for a light gel-cream, The Ordinary NMF + Beta Glucan for an oil-free gel, or Sebamed Clear Face Care Gel for a simple water-based option. Niacinamide is absent from the official ingredient lists checked for this guide; confirm the list on the exact product you buy.

Finding a lightweight moisturizer can be surprisingly frustrating when you want to avoid niacinamide. It appears in plenty of products aimed at oily skin, even when it is not the ingredient you are shopping for. Fortunately, hydration does not require it.

This guide is for readers who prefer a niacinamide-free routine or are simplifying their product choices. It does not assume that niacinamide is harmful, or that a reaction to one serum proves an allergy to the ingredient. The goal is to find a comfortable moisturizer with a texture you want to use.

We compared the brands' published ingredient lists, texture descriptions, intended uses and product formats. The selections are editorial judgments, not clinical rankings or hands-on test results. Prices, availability and formulas can change; check your local listing and the package before buying.

Best gel-cream for combination skin

1. Geek & Gorgeous Hydration Station

Texture: Light gel-cream
Size: 50 mL

Hydration Station is the first pick if you want lightness with a little more cushioning than a clear gel. Its current ingredient list includes glycerin, ectoin, emollients, bisabolol and allantoin, without niacinamide or added fragrance. That combination makes it an appealing middle ground for a simple routine. 1

Shop Geek & Gorgeous Hydration Station ↗

Buy it if: Choose it if your cheeks want some softness but a rich cream feels excessive across your T-zone. It is a sensible place to start when you want one uncomplicated moisturizer.

Consider another option if: Skip it if you are looking for a thick winter cream or a heavily occlusive finish. “Without niacinamide” does not tell you how rich a moisturizer will feel.

Best oil-free gel

2. The Ordinary Natural Moisturizing Factors + Beta Glucan

Texture: Lightweight gel
Size: 30 mL or 100 mL; select the Beta Glucan version

The Beta Glucan version is an oil-free gel with humectants, amino acids and beta-glucan. The published ingredient list does not include niacinamide. It is the most straightforward choice here if “light gel” is the texture you actually want, rather than a cream that merely claims to feel light. 2

Shop The Ordinary Natural Moisturizing Factors + Beta Glucan ↗

Buy it if: Choose it for oily or combination skin when you prefer a minimal-feeling layer. The smaller size lets you try the texture before committing to a larger tube.

Consider another option if: Skip it if you need a rich final layer. Check the exact name: Natural Moisturizing Factors + HA and Natural Moisturizing Factors + PhytoCeramides are different products, not alternate sizes of this gel.

Best simple water-based gel

3. Sebamed Clear Face Care Gel

Texture: Clear, lightweight gel
Size: 50 mL; choose Care Gel, not the cleanser or toner

Sebamed offers a relatively short ingredient list built around water-binding ingredients, including glycerin, panthenol and sodium hyaluronate, plus allantoin and aloe. Niacinamide is absent from the published list. This is a useful option if you prefer a clear gel and want to keep your moisturizer choice easy to check. 3

Shop Sebamed Clear Face Care Gel ↗

Buy it if: Choose it when heavy creams feel uncomfortable and you mainly want a light hydrating layer. Its small tube is also convenient for a pared-back travel routine.

Consider another option if: Skip it as your only moisturizer if your skin is very dry or flaky. Despite the Clear Face branding, treat it as a moisturizer; it is not a substitute for an acne treatment.

Which texture makes sense for your skin?

Oily T-zone, cheeks that need softness: start with the gel-cream option, Hydration Station. A strong preference for an oil-free gel: look at The Ordinary. The simplest clear-gel approach: consider Sebamed. These are editorial matches based on the formulas and product formats, not results from a head-to-head wear test.

If your face is flaky, tight and uncomfortable despite looking shiny, a very light gel may not give you enough comfort. Do not let the words “oily skin” push you into buying the thinnest possible product. Your cheeks and T-zone can also need different amounts.

Start with one moisturizer and avoid changing every step at once. That gives you a clearer sense of how the new formula fits. You do not need to add another serum just to compensate for a moisturizer that does not suit your needs.

How to check a niacinamide-free purchase

  1. Match the full product name, not only the brand.
  2. Read the ingredient list on the exact regional listing and on the package when it arrives.
  3. Check for “niacinamide”; do not assume every product in the same range uses the same formula.
  4. Try a new product on a small area before wider use, and stop if it causes a reaction.

Ingredient lists and packaging change. The three lists linked below did not include niacinamide when checked on September 25, 2026. That is a snapshot of the published formulas, not a guarantee for every future batch.

Does oily skin still need moisturizer?

The American Academy of Dermatology recommends moisturizing even when skin is oily. You can choose a lighter texture without abandoning the step. A product's overall feel and your response are more useful shopping signals than trying to find one perfect hero ingredient. 4

If many unrelated products suddenly sting, buying another active-heavy product may make the routine harder to assess. Simplify first, and seek professional advice for a persistent rash or burning rather than trying to diagnose an ingredient allergy from online lists.

Quick questions

Does niacinamide-free mean irritation-free?

No. Any finished formula can disagree with an individual. Avoiding one ingredient does not guarantee that every other ingredient will suit you.

Are all The Ordinary moisturizers the same?

No. This recommendation is specifically Natural Moisturizing Factors + Beta Glucan. The + HA and + PhytoCeramides products are different formulas.

Which one should I try first?

Choose Hydration Station for a gel-cream feel, The Ordinary for an oil-free gel, or Sebamed for a simple clear gel. You only need one; start with the texture that best matches your preference.

Your next step

Choose the product that matches your routine and texture preference, then use its shopping button to check the current listing. You do not need the whole shortlist to build a good routine.
